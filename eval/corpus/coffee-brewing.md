# Brewing Coffee at Home

A practical guide to making consistently good coffee with simple equipment.

## Grind Size

Grind size controls how fast water extracts flavor. Espresso needs a very fine grind, similar to powdered sugar. Pour-over works best with a medium-fine grind like table salt. French press needs a coarse grind, similar to sea salt, so the metal filter does not clog.

## Water Temperature

The ideal brewing temperature is between 90 and 96 degrees Celsius. Boiling water over-extracts and makes coffee bitter. If you do not have a thermometer, let boiled water rest for about 30 seconds before pouring.

## Coffee to Water Ratio

A good starting ratio is 1 gram of coffee for every 16 grams of water. For a single 250 ml cup, use about 15 grams of ground coffee. Increase the coffee for a stronger cup rather than steeping longer.

## Storing Beans

Keep whole beans in an airtight, opaque container at room temperature. Do not store beans in the refrigerator, because moisture and odors ruin the flavor. Beans taste best within four weeks of the roast date, and you should grind them only right before brewing.
